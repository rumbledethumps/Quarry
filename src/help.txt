Quarry - an incremental brick breaker.
Move: A/D, arrows, keypad 4/6, or a gamepad.
Launch: Space, Enter, up, keypad 0, or A/B/X/Y.
Pause: Esc or Start.
The energy bar drains from the first launch. When it is empty,
the paddle falls and the stage is over. Broken bricks turn into
nuggets, counted in the left panel. Spend them on upgrades
when the stage is over. The game saves as you go.
